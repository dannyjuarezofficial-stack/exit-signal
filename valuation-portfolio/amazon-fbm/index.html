What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Fulfillment economics

Shipping labor, warehouse costs and delivery performance can materially change true profit.

Product concentration

A narrow catalog can increase dependence on a small number of listings or suppliers.

Operational complexity

The more the founder personally manages fulfillment and support, the harder the transition can be.

Amazon dependence

Marketplace performance, reviews and account standing remain central to buyer risk.

How is FBM different from FBA for buyers?

FBM puts more fulfillment responsibility inside the business, so logistics and owner workload can matter more.

Can mixed FBA and FBM businesses be valued?

Mixed models may require a broader valuation discussion. This page is intentionally locked to the FBM category.

Does shipping cost affect value?

Yes. Sustainable margin after fulfillment costs is more useful than revenue alone.
