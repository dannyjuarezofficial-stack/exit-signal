What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Recurring revenue

Retainers and repeatable contracts can be easier for buyers to model than project-only revenue.

Client concentration

A business that depends heavily on one or two clients can carry more transfer risk.

Founder-led sales

If most new business depends on the owner's relationships, a buyer may discount the handoff risk.

Team and delivery

Documented service delivery, capable staff and clear margins can support transferability.

Questions

Before you value it

Should I use gross billings or real agency revenue?

Pass-through media or contractor spend can distort headline revenue. Buyers usually care about the economics the agency actually keeps.

Can a founder-led agency be sold?

Yes, but founder dependence can affect risk, transition planning and buyer appetite.

Does this replace a professional valuation?

No. The tool is an estimate and a starting point for a possible sale conversation.
