    Before you continue

    Common questions

    Does Exit Signal decide whether Empire Flippers will accept my business?

    No. Exit Signal can help you identify obvious readiness issues. Acceptance and valuation decisions belong to Empire Flippers.

    Do I have to give Exit Signal my email or financials?

    No. The readiness check runs in your browser. If you choose to open the Empire Flippers valuation tool, information entered there is handled by Empire Flippers.

    Why not just go straight to a broker?

    You can. Exit Signal exists for owners who want to understand whether a sale conversation makes sense before starting one.

    What if the check says “prepare first”?

    Use the stated blocker or drag as your first repair target. Fixing it first can keep a known issue from following you into a sale process.
