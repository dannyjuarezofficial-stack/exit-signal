How Exit Signal makes money

Exit Signal has a referral relationship with Empire Flippers.

If you use an Exit Signal Empire Flippers link or the embedded Empire Flippers valuation tool and later complete an eligible transaction with them, they may pay us a referral fee under their program terms. You do not pay anything extra because you came through Exit Signal.

What this does not change

  • The readiness check’s rules are published in full on the methodology page.
  • Compensation does not change the readiness result.
  • Exit Signal does not decide whether you qualify or what your business is worth. Empire Flippers makes those decisions.
  • Where a different route may fit better, Exit Signal can say so.
