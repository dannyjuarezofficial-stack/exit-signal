Privacy

The V1 readiness check runs in your browser. It does not require an email address and does not ask you to upload tax returns, bank statements, leases, utility bills or financial documents.

Aggregate interaction events may be measured, such as check start, step reach, completion and result category. Those events must not include your selected financial answers, free text, names, email addresses or financial documents.
