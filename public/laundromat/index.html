    Common questions

    What this check does and does not do

    Does this tell me what my laundromat is worth?

    No. It identifies sale-readiness issues. A valuation requires current financials, transaction context and often professional judgment.

    Why not just call a broker?

    You can. This check is for owners who want to understand obvious preparation issues before choosing a sale route.

    Do you collect my financial documents?

    No. V1 does not ask you to upload tax returns, bank statements, leases or utility bills.

    Where do the rules come from?

    See the methodology page. The rules are intentionally conservative and based on published buyer/seller diligence guidance.
