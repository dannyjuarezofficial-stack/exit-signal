Methodology

How the readiness check decides

The check uses ten plain questions and a published rule order. It does not use a statistical model. It does not produce a score.

Result order

  1. Too early to tell if the business is under 12 months old, or is breaking even or losing money.
  2. Need more information if a critical answer is “Not sure”. Two or more unknown answers also produce this result.
  3. Prepare first if any answer could stop a sale, or if two or more answers drag the price.
  4. Ready to explore otherwise.

The timing question never changes the reading. It only changes how much preparation time may be available.

When the valuation path appears

The Empire Flippers valuation path appears for Ready to explore. It can also appear for Prepare first. In that case, the seller must report at least 12 months of history and at least $2,000 a month in average net profit. An active Amazon account-health problem blocks the path. Meeting these conditions does not mean Empire Flippers will accept the business.

Privacy of the readiness answers

The readiness answers stay in your browser in this candidate build and are not transmitted to Exit Signal. The separate Empire Flippers valuation tool is operated by Empire Flippers and follows their data practices.
