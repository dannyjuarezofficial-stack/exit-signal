What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Engaged audience

Subscriber count matters less when readers do not open, click or respond.

Revenue mix

Advertising, sponsorships, paid subscriptions and products create different durability profiles.

Acquisition sources

An audience built from repeatable channels can be easier to grow after transfer.

Creator dependence

A newsletter centered on one personality can require a more careful transition than a brand-led publication.

Questions

Before you value it

Does subscriber count determine value?

No. Engagement, earnings and audience quality usually matter more than list size alone.

Can a personal newsletter be sold?

Potentially, but the more value depends on the writer's identity, the more transition risk a buyer may see.

Does paid subscription revenue help?

Recurring revenue can be attractive when retention and subscriber quality are strong.
