What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Profit quality

Buyers care about earnings they can verify and understand, especially after normal operating expenses.

Customer and traffic mix

Heavy dependence on one acquisition channel or one customer segment can add risk.

Products and suppliers

Product concentration, supplier durability and fulfillment complexity affect how transferable the business feels.

Owner dependence

Documented operations and a business that can run without constant founder involvement are easier for a buyer to take over.

Questions

Before you value it

Is this only for Shopify stores?

The page is aimed at established direct-to-consumer eCommerce businesses. The official Empire Flippers tool determines the fields it needs for the eCommerce monetization.

Do I need to be ready to sell?

No. Empire Flippers says it is reasonable to get a valuation even when you are not currently planning to sell.

Is the estimate the final sale price?

No. Empire Flippers states that an automated valuation is not necessarily the listing price or eventual sale price because a full sale process can consider additional factors.
