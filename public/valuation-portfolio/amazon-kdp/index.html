What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Catalog durability

A broad catalog with durable demand can reduce dependence on one title or trend.

Rights and ownership

Clear ownership of manuscripts, covers and other publishing assets matters in diligence.

Royalty stability

Consistent royalty history can be more persuasive than a short-lived spike.

Advertising dependence

Buyer risk rises when sales require unusually heavy or fragile paid promotion.

Questions

Before you value it

Can a KDP business be sold?

Empire Flippers maintains a dedicated KDP category, but transfer structure and account details should be handled carefully during a real transaction.

Do individual books matter?

Yes. Buyers can care about title concentration, rankings and how durable demand appears.

Should I include unpublished assets?

A buyer may value transferable intellectual property, but the automated estimate is only a starting point.
