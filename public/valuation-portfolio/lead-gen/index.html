What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Lead quality

Buyers care whether leads consistently convert for end customers, not only how many forms are submitted.

Client concentration

Dependence on one lead buyer can make revenue more fragile.

Traffic durability

SEO, paid media and local assets carry different acquisition risks.

Routing and operations

Tracking, call routing, forms and fulfillment should keep working after ownership changes.
