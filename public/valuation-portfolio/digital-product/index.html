What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Product concentration

A business with several durable offers can be less fragile than one built around a single launch.

Audience ownership

Email lists, direct traffic and repeat customers can reduce reliance on one platform.

Refund and support load

High refund rates or heavy founder support can reduce the attractiveness of reported revenue.

Creator dependence

Products that rely on the founder's identity or constant personal delivery can be harder to transfer.

What counts as a digital product?

Examples can include downloadable products and other non-physical offers. The exact business classification belongs with Empire Flippers.

Do launches distort value?

They can. Buyers often care about how repeatable earnings are outside one launch window.

Can a creator-led product sell?

Potentially, but dependence on the creator can change transition risk and buyer appetite.
