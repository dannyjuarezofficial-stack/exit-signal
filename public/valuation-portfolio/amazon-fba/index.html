What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Profit quality

Buyers want clean earnings after advertising, fees, refunds and normal operating costs.

SKU concentration

A business dominated by one product can be more exposed to ranking, supply or competitive shocks.

Supply chain

Supplier durability, lead times and inventory planning affect how smoothly the business can transfer.

Account health

Unresolved compliance or marketplace issues can materially change buyer confidence.
