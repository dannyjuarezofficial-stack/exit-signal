What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Traffic durability

Buyers look closely at where traffic comes from, how concentrated it is and how exposed it may be to search or platform changes.

Revenue concentration

Dependence on one affiliate program, one merchant or a narrow group of pages can increase risk.

Content and authority

A defensible content library, durable rankings and a recognizable audience can make the asset easier to underwrite.

Owner workload

Repeatable publishing, updating and monetization processes make the business easier to transfer.
