What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Profit after ads

Revenue can look impressive while customer-acquisition cost absorbs the margin.

Supplier reliability

Product quality, lead times and supplier relationships affect whether the business can survive a transfer.

Traffic dependence

A store built entirely on one paid channel can be more volatile than one with several acquisition sources.

Operational handoff

Support, refunds, creative production and supplier management should be repeatable without the founder.
