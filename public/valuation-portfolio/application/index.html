What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Active users and retention

Downloads alone say less than whether users keep returning and generating value.

Revenue model

Subscriptions, ads and in-app purchases create different risk and margin profiles.

Platform standing

App-store policy compliance, reviews and account status can affect transferability.

Code and backend handoff

A buyer needs understandable source code, infrastructure and operating documentation.

Questions

Before you value it

Is this for SaaS apps?

This page is for mobile-app businesses. A web SaaS product has its own dedicated valuation category.

Do downloads determine value?

Not by themselves. Revenue, retention and operating quality are usually more important than download count alone.

Can app-store listings transfer?

Apple and Google both provide formal transfer processes subject to their requirements.
