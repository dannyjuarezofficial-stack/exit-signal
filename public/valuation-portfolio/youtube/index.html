What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

View consistency

Stable audience demand across many videos is easier to underwrite than one viral spike.

Revenue mix

AdSense, sponsorships, affiliates and products create different risk and margin profiles.

Host dependence

A faceless or transferable format may be easier for a buyer to continue than a channel tied entirely to one personality.

Platform standing

Policy compliance, copyright history and channel health matter in diligence.

Questions

Before you value it

Do subscribers determine value?

No. Views, profit, trend direction and audience quality usually matter more than subscriber count by itself.

Can a personality-led channel be sold?

Potentially, but the transfer can be harder when the audience primarily follows the creator.

Does one viral video increase value?

It can increase earnings, but buyers usually look for durable performance across the channel.
