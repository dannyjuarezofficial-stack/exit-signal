What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Search and traffic mix

Heavy dependence on a narrow group of rankings can make future earnings less predictable.

Commission concentration

Changes to Amazon's program or a small number of product categories can materially affect revenue.

Content portfolio

A diversified library of useful pages can reduce dependence on any single article or keyword cluster.

Operational handoff

Editorial systems, tracking and content maintenance should be understandable to a new owner.
