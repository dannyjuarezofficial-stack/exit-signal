What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Retention

Recurring revenue becomes more valuable when customers stay long enough to create predictable economics.

Acquisition efficiency

Customer-acquisition cost matters when growth requires continual paid spend.

Fulfillment burden

Physical or service delivery can change margins and operating complexity.

Revenue concentration

A diversified subscriber base can be easier for buyers to model and transfer.

Questions

Before you value it

Is every recurring business SaaS?

No. Subscription is broader and can include recurring models that are not software.

Does churn reduce value?

High churn can make future revenue less predictable and force expensive replacement acquisition.

Should I use Subscription Box instead?

If the core model is a physical subscription box, use the dedicated Subscription-Box category.
