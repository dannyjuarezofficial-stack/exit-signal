What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Client concentration

A broad customer base can reduce the risk that one lost client damages earnings.

Recurring work

Contracts and repeatable service relationships can make future revenue easier to model.

Delivery margins

A buyer needs to understand labor, contractor and fulfillment costs behind reported revenue.

Owner dependence

If the founder performs most delivery or relationship management, the handoff can be harder.
