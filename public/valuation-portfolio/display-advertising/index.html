What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Traffic sources

Search, direct, social and referral traffic have different durability and concentration risks.

Content moat

Original utility and durable audience demand can matter more than a large page count.

Revenue efficiency

Ad RPMs and the mix of monetized geographies affect the quality of earnings.

Algorithm exposure

A business that can survive search or platform changes is generally easier to underwrite.

Before you value it

Is traffic the main valuation driver?

Traffic matters because it produces revenue, but buyers also care about profit, trend direction and durability.

Do AI and search changes matter?

Yes. Traffic-source risk is a major consideration for content-driven businesses.

Does this include affiliate revenue?

This page is for display advertising. Affiliate businesses have separate valuation categories.
