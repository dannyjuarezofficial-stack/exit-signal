What buyers look at

Value depends on more than revenue.

Subscriber retention

A healthy recurring cohort is more persuasive than gross signups without retention.

Gross margin

Product cost, packaging, shipping and fulfillment materially affect true profitability.

Inventory and sourcing

Reliable supply and manageable inventory reduce operational risk for a buyer.

Acquisition economics

Paid acquisition can work well only when lifetime value supports the cost to win subscribers.
