About Exit Signal

Exit Signal helps established Amazon FBA and private-label owners decide whether a sale makes sense now or whether something should be fixed first.

It exists because an owner may want a useful decision before starting a broker conversation. Exit Signal would rather tell you that it is too early than send you somewhere you are likely to be turned down.

Exit Signal was started by Danny Juarez. It is a small independent operation. Exit Signal does not broker or buy businesses. It is not a marketplace or appraisal service. It is not part of Empire Flippers or Amazon. It does not provide legal or tax advice.

How we are paid

Empire Flippers may pay Exit Signal a referral fee when an eligible referred transaction closes. See How Exit Signal makes money.
