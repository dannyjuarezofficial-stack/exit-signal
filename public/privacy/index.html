Privacy

Exit Signal is operated by Enrique Daniel Juarez, doing business as Exit Signal, as a sole proprietor.

The readiness check

The Exit Signal readiness check runs in your browser. You do not need to give Exit Signal an email address to see a result. Your readiness answers are not sent to Exit Signal.

The Empire Flippers valuation tool

If you choose to open the embedded Empire Flippers valuation tool, it loads software and services operated by Empire Flippers. Information you enter into that tool is handled by Empire Flippers and is subject to their privacy practices. The embed includes Exit Signal’s referral identifier so Empire Flippers can attribute eligible referrals.

Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process your IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site. It also processes the page you request.

Analytics

Exit Signal uses Cloudflare Zaraz for aggregate interaction measurement. It may record events such as quiz start and completion, result category, valuation-tool opening, Empire Flippers outbound clicks, scroll-depth milestones and engaged-time milestones. These events do not include your quiz answers, names, email addresses, free text or financial values.

Email

If you email Exit Signal, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. Contact: danny@getexitsignal.com.
